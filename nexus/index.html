Agents were still the bridge between every system. So I built Nexus. Not for a pitch deck. For my agents, and for their clients.

Questions

The honest answers

I barely use AI. Is this over my head?

If you can type a sentence, you can use Nexus. And you don’t set it up alone. Every plan includes a personal onboarding session where we connect everything with you and run your first workflow.

I’m not techy. Who connects everything?

We do, with you, live on your onboarding call. You authorize your tools; we handle the rest. No code, no IT department, no YouTube tutorials at midnight.

What AI do I need?

Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or another AI assistant you already use, free or paid. If you’re not using one yet, we’ll help you pick during onboarding.

Is my client data safe?

Nexus connects through your own accounts’ official authorizations. Your data stays in your CRM and your tools. Nexus is the bridge, not a new database your clients live in.

What CRM do I need?

Nexus supports Follow Up Boss, BoldTrail, Lofty, GoHighLevel, BoomTown, and SureSend. Bring yours to the working session. If it can’t connect, we’ll tell you straight.

What does a Nexus engagement include?

A personal onboarding session, connection to your CRM and AI assistant, and your first workflows running live. We recommend a plan after learning about your tools and goals on your AI Strategy Call.
