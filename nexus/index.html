The Problem

Your tools don't talk to each other.
So you became the messenger.

You've got a CRM, email, a calendar, property data, marketing tools, and an AI that's smart enough to help with all of it, except it can't touch any of it. So every workflow runs through you: open the CRM, find the contact, copy the history, paste it into the AI, copy the answer back, send it yourself, update the record yourself.


Nexus removes you as the middleman. One connection, and your AI can read your CRM, research the property, send the follow-up, and set the next task, while you stay focused on clients.

"I built Nexus for my 19 agents. Now I'm opening it to the industry. 25 beta spots, and I personally onboard every single one."

Nexus was not built by developers who studied real estate from a weekend webinar. It was built from inside a working real estate business, by someone who has spent 26 years in the industry — in sales, in foreclosure law, in housing counseling, in team leadership.

When AI became capable enough to actually do things — not just suggest things — I saw the same problem I'd always seen in this industry: the technology existed, but nobody had wired it together the way real estate actually works. Agents were still the bridge between every system. The AI was just another island.

So I built Nexus. Not for a pitch deck. Not for a Series A. For my agents. For their clients. For the distressed homeowner who needs a warm letter in their mailbox 72 hours after an NOD files — not three weeks later because the agent's workflow couldn't move fast enough.

That's who Nexus is built by. And that's who it's built for.

Questions

The honest answers.

I barely use AI. Is this over my head?

If you can type a sentence, you can use Nexus. And you don't set it up alone. Every plan includes a personal working session where we connect everything with you and run your first workflow before the call ends.

I'm not techy. Who connects everything?

We do, with you, live on your onboarding call. You authorize your tools; we handle the rest. No code, no IT department, no YouTube tutorials at midnight.

What AI do I need?

Claude, ChatGPT, or Perplexity, free or paid. If you're not using one yet, we'll help you pick during onboarding.

Is my client data safe?

Nexus connects through your own accounts' official authorizations. Your data stays in your CRM and your tools. Nexus is the bridge, not a new database your clients live in.

What CRM do I need?

Nexus works with the major real estate CRMs and team/brokerage platforms. Bring yours to the working session. If it can't connect, we'll tell you straight, and the session costs you nothing.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Pro is $30/month for your first 3 paid months, then $49, and we remind you at least 7 days before that first $49 charge. Cancel anytime during the trial and pay nothing.
