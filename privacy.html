Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information Elevate Ops 360 collects through this website and what happens to it. Questions? Email hello@elevateops360.com.

The homepage

Our homepage does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or sign-up forms, and it loads its fonts and images from our own site. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

Information you give us

Some other pages on this site have forms (for example event registration, training registration, update sign-ups and client onboarding). Depending on the form, we may collect your name, email address, mobile number, business name and the answers you choose to give. We use this to respond to you, run the event or service you signed up for, and follow up about it. Form submissions are processed by Formspree, a third-party form service.

Booking a call

The booking button opens a Calendly page. Calendly collects the details you enter to schedule the call and handles them under its own privacy policy. We receive your booking details so we can meet with you.

Referral links and checkout

On the Nexus page, if you arrive through a referral link, we record the referral click on our server and save a small identifier in your browser (local storage) for up to 30 days, so that a purchase can be credited to the right referrer. Purchases are made on Stripe-hosted checkout pages; we do not see your card number.

Hosting and technical data

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address and browser type. We do not control those logs.

Third-party links

Links to other sites, including Marblism, Calendly and Stripe, are governed by those companies' own policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it, email hello@elevateops360.com. You can clear the saved referral identifier at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and its date.
